# Stylist 6 AI taste admission, 17 November 2026

The initial photo-informed Director could still only pick among combinations
already stripped of garments by a long list of stylistic title heuristics.
This follow-up removes the root failure for isolated, server-flagged catalog
AI mode: Fashion Director can inspect safe, complete alternatives without
the old rulebook deciding what "looks elegant" beforehand.

Model mode still enforces real SKU/ownership, document-backed stock/merchant,
user exclusions (colors/patterns and pinned anchors), verified price/budget,
actual outfit slot structure, workout requirements and cold-weather safety.
Formal black-tie attire remains strict. Wardrobe and mixed modes and any
AI-degraded fallback retain their earlier editorial behavior.

Our independently hosted photo critic still makes the final admission decision.
A GREEN CI or approved model score is not proof of independent human fashion
quality; authenticated real-image tests and blind human review remain mandatory.
