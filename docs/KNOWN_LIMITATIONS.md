# KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md (Phase 7BQ)

Honest limits. Shown in product where material, never silently worked around.

1. Limited merchant coverage; unsupported URLs fall back to manual add.
2. Price history unavailable for some products — stated, never fabricated.
3. Fit confidence depends on evidence; low evidence never yields a guarantee.
4. VTON is visualization, not fit proof.
5. Visual parsing may need correction; user correction always wins.
6. No-buy advice is product-value-driven and unaffected by affiliate revenue.
7. Offline mode: cached wardrobe + saved outfits; refresh-dependent data is
   labeled unavailable, not faked.
8. Weekly summaries count only verified savings with provenance.
9. Social proof appears only with n≥50 valid cohort members.
10. New-environment baselines (cost, latency, scale) are unknown until the
    first post-launch windows are measured.
