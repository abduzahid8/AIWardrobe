Privacy Policy

How AIWardrobe protects your personal information

Last updated: April 30, 2026

AIWardrobe ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

By using AIWardrobe, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and authentication credentials when you create an account
  • Profile Information: Style preferences, body measurements (optional), and fashion preferences you share
  • Wardrobe Content: Photos of clothing items you upload to organize your virtual closet
  • Communications: Messages you send to our AI stylist or support team

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: How you interact with the app, features used, session duration
  • App Performance: Crash logs, error reports, and performance diagnostics

1.3 Optional Permissions

With your permission, we may access:

  • Camera: To take photos of clothing items for your wardrobe
  • Photos: To select and upload clothing images from your library
  • Notifications: To send style suggestions and app updates

2. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Information Used
Provide AI styling recommendations Wardrobe photos, style preferences, profile data
Virtual try-on features Uploaded photos, selected garment images
Personalize your experience Usage patterns, style preferences, interactions
Process subscriptions Apple ID transaction data (via Apple's system)
Improve our services Usage analytics, crash reports, feedback
Communicate with you Email address, notification tokens

3. AI Processing and Data

Important Information About AI Features

  • Photos you upload for AI try-on or analysis may be processed by third-party AI services (NVIDIA, Fashn.ai, Replicate)
  • These services process images solely to generate results and do not retain your data for training
  • We use industry-standard encryption for all data transfers
  • You retain full ownership of all photos and content you upload

4. Data Storage and Security

  • Your data is stored on secure servers provided by Supabase (AWS infrastructure)
  • All data transfers use TLS/SSL encryption
  • Wardrobe photos are stored in encrypted cloud storage
  • We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data
  • Access to user data is restricted to authorized personnel only

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services
  • RevenueCat: Subscription management and in-app purchases
  • Replicate/Runway/NVIDIA: AI image generation for try-on features
  • Apple App Store: Payment processing for subscriptions

These services have their own privacy policies and are compliant with applicable data protection regulations.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update or correct your information
  • Deletion: Delete your account and associated data
  • Portability: Export your wardrobe data
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics and marketing communications

How to Exercise Your Rights

  • Update profile information in the app's Profile settings
  • Delete your account via Profile → Delete Account
  • Contact us at support@aiwardrobe.club for data export requests
  • Manage iOS permissions in your device Settings

7. Children's Privacy

AIWardrobe is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at support@aiwardrobe.club.

8. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Wardrobe photos: Retained until you delete them or your account
  • Usage analytics: Anonymized after 12 months
  • Subscription records: Retained as required by law (typically 7 years)

9. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers, including standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions where applicable.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes through the app or by email. The "Last updated" date at the top of this policy indicates when it was most recently revised.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us:

Email: support@aiwardrobe.club

We aim to respond to all privacy-related inquiries within 48 hours.
