  • Use the "Manage Subscription" button in the app's Profile screen

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime through your Apple ID settings: Go to iOS Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → AIWardrobe → Cancel Subscription.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the AIWardrobe app, go to the upgrade screen, and tap "Restore Purchases." Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Is my data secure?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption and your photos are processed securely. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

What features are included in Pro/Max?

Pro and Max subscriptions unlock unlimited AI outfit suggestions, virtual try-on, AI stylist chat, wardrobe analytics, trip planner, and more. See the app for full details.
